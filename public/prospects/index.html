How this list was built (v2)

  1. Start from activity — Yelp “Updated July 2026”, BirdEye/Google review aggregates, Facebook posts 2025–26, Reddit, chamber/BBB still listed.
  2. Then check the website — Weebly/Wix free, no viewport, template/placeholder, Comcast/AOL email, missing quote UX, dated layout.
  3. Skip pure zombies — No recent reviews + abandoned free host = out (that was v1’s failure mode).
  4. Confidence tag — HIGH = multiple live signals; MED = some live signals, call before building a full demo; LOW = thin evidence, phone-screen only.
Still not perfect. I cannot open Google Maps review dates for every listing from here. Before you spend a day on a demo: (1) open their Google Business Profile, (2) check a review from the last 6–12 months, (3) call once. If the line is dead, kill the lead.
