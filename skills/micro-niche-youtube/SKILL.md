# Micro Niche YouTube

Acquire high-ticket buyers and sell specialized info products from a low-view, high-intent YouTube channel using targeted search optimization and backend sales funnels.

## Features

- **Qualified Market Targeting**: Skip mass appeal; map your content strategy to the active search queries of high-intent buyers.
- **Three-Tier Video Funnel**: Structure your video pipeline to educate, build familiarity, and drive calls/sales.
- **DM & Calendar Booking Funnel**: Turn viewers into leads by directing them to custom landing pages, social DMs, or booking links.

## Workflows

### 1. Identify Your Micro-Niche & ICP
Focus your channel on a highly specific audience segment:
1. **Narrow down the topic**: Instead of general "SEO strategies," target "Programmatic SEO for B2B SaaS Founders."
2. **Find expensive problems**: List 5 specific, high-friction issues that your ICP would pay $1,000+ to solve immediately.
3. **Map search intent**: Use search suggestion tools to list exact phrases your ICP types when looking for help.

### 2. Structure Your Content Flywheel
Plan a balanced content mix across three layers of buyer awareness:
- **Top of Funnel (TOF - 50%)**: Contrarian rants, case studies, or conceptual guides explaining the "what" and "why" of the problem.
- **Middle of Funnel (MOF - 30%)**: Step-by-step tutorial walkthroughs and tool comparisons demonstrating the "how."
- **Bottom of Funnel (BOF - 20%)**: Detailed client result interviews, video sales letters (VSLs), or specific offer details.

## Playbook Checklist

- [ ] **Step 1: Channel Positioning**
  - Define the single, hyper-targeted niche of the channel.
  - Optimize the channel bio, links, and banner to address the primary customer pain point.
- [ ] **Step 2: Video Production**
  - Draft a script for a 15-30 minute high-value guide.
  - Record with clean audio, leading directly with the solution (avoiding long generic intros).
  - Add a clear call-to-action redirecting viewers to a resources link in the description.
- [ ] **Step 3: Funnel Setup**
  - Create the landing page or booking link used in the video description.
  - Connect analytics to track CTR from YouTube views to link clicks and calls booked.
- [ ] **Step 4: Repurposing**
  - Extract 3 short clips from the main video to use as reels/shorts/X posts.
